Rules for accurate, safe and reviewable map data.

  • ناوی کوردی، عەرەبی و ئینگلیزی لە خانەی زمانەکەی خۆی بنووسە؛ ناوی ساختە مەنووسە.

إرشادات عربية

  • أضف أماكن حقيقية فقط مع إحداثيات وأسماء موثوقة.
  • ضع الاسم الكردي والعربي والإنجليزي في الحقل الصحيح ولا تخترع ترجمة.
  • ارفع صورًا تملكها أو تملك إذنًا لنشرها.
  • المكان الجديد والتعديل على مكان منشور يدخلان المراجعة.
  • يمكنك حذف المكان الذي أضفته مباشرة دون مراجعة الحذف.

1. Accuracy and provenance

Use the most precise truthful category, coordinate and administrative context available. Do not add fictional, joke, placeholder, promotional spam, duplicate or test records. When a translation is unknown, leave it empty rather than inventing one.

2. Language quality

Keep Kurdish, Arabic and English fields distinct. Use correct Kurdish characters and local spellings where verified. Add aliases only when they are genuine alternate, historical or official names.

3. Photos and rights

Upload only images you own or are authorized to share. Do not expose faces, vehicle plates, private homes, documents or other personal information without a lawful basis. Pending media remains private until an approved publication workflow promotes it.

4. Moderation lifecycle

  1. New submission: pending and visible only to you and administrators.
  2. Approval: published and an in-app notification is sent.
  3. Rejection: remains non-public; you receive an in-app notification and may correct it.
  4. Edit after approval: a separate pending revision is created; the current approved version stays public.
  5. Edit approval/rejection: you receive an in-app notification.
  6. Delete your own place: permanent deletion is performed by the authenticated server function without a deletion review queue.

5. Safety-sensitive information

Do not publish confidential critical-infrastructure details, precise private-person locations, credentials, access codes or material whose publication creates a credible security, privacy or legal risk. Public checkpoint/border data must be sourced and categorized carefully.

6. Abuse and correction

Administrators may rate-limit, reject, hide or remove low-quality or abusive submissions. Map inaccuracies may be reported through the in-app Feedback section or by email to sarhang.salah9@gmail.com.

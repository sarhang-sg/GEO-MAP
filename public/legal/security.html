In scope

  • Authentication and authorization bypass affecting NAV KURD.
  • Exposure of non-public user contributions, moderation data or credentials.
  • Cross-site scripting, request forgery, injection or server-function abuse with a reproducible impact.
  • Security-control bypasses in the production origin.

Out of scope

  • Automated scanner findings without a reproducible security impact.
  • Denial-of-service or excessive traffic tests.
  • Social engineering, phishing or attacks against third-party providers.
  • Public geographic-data accuracy disputes that are not security vulnerabilities.

Safe testing

Use only accounts and data you own. Do not access, alter, delete or publish another person’s data. Avoid disruption and stop testing if you encounter sensitive information. Include the affected URL, browser/device, time, proof of concept and suggested remediation when possible.

Response

Reports are reviewed in good faith. Acknowledgement and remediation timing depend on severity, reproducibility and third-party dependencies. This policy does not authorize unlawful activity and is not a promise of a bounty.

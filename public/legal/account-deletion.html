تعليمات عربية

من قسم الحساب والمساهمات سجّل الدخول بالحساب نفسه عبر Google واختر حذف الحساب والبيانات. بعد التأكيد تنفذ دالة خادمية موثوقة الحذف مباشرة، ولا ينتظر الطلب موافقة المشرف.

In-app steps

  1. Open NAV KURD and choose Account & contributions.
  2. Sign in through Google with the account to be deleted.
  3. Select the account-and-data deletion action.
  4. Read the warning and explicitly confirm permanent deletion.

What is deleted

The trusted Edge Function removes account-owned contributions, revisions, notifications, profile data, pending media and the Supabase authentication user, subject to successful server-side integrity checks. You are signed out after completion.

What may remain temporarily

Non-personal security/audit records, provider backups and previously generated public map releases may persist for limited operational, integrity or legal reasons. A public geographic fact that no longer contains an account identifier is not necessarily personal account data.

Delete a place without deleting the account

You may permanently delete a place you submitted from your contribution dashboard. This authenticated deletion does not need administrator review. Editing an approved place is different: the edit enters review while the current approved version remains public.
